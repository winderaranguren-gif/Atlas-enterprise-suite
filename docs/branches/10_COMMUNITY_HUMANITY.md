# Branch 10 — Community & Humanity

**Parent:** ATLAS Master Index & Genealogy  
**Lineage:** Human mission → ATLAS OS / Core → Community & Humanity  
**Status:** living branch dossier  
**Rule:** a person asking for help is not a sales lead. Participant, volunteer, donor/sponsor, provider/partner and staff identities may be linked when consent and purpose require it, but their roles, access and data boundaries remain distinct.

---

## 1. Branch purpose

Community & Humanity turns fragmented needs/resources into dignified navigation and accountable follow-through. Its fruit is a person reaching a verified next step, a volunteer serving safely, a donation being traceable, a partner handoff being acknowledged or an impact result being measured without exposing private participant histories.

## 2. Roots inherited

- Dignity before optimization.
- Inclusion regardless of nationality, origin, language, migration status or economic condition.
- Minimum necessary data and consent.
- Need does not create commercial marketing rights.
- Referrals complement qualified professionals and public/community institutions; ATLAS is not their replacement.
- Sensitive health/social-service data is not collected simply because it could be useful.
- Follow-up is purpose-bound and opt-in where appropriate.
- Individual records and aggregate impact reporting remain separated.
- Legal/tax status must be truthful before donation/tax-deductibility claims.

## 3. Trunk dependencies

Identity/Auth, Organization/DBA, RBAC, Audit Ledger, People identities, Forms, Documents, Knowledge, Operations/Appointments, Inventory, Finance, Global Context, accessibility, provider/resource adapters and privacy/consent controls.

## 4. Canonical sub-branches

### 4.1 United Hands / Community Recovery Hub — ARTIFACT-VERIFIED CONCEPT FRUIT

Historical proposals define intake, consent, needs, appointments, hygiene/support coordination, donation inventory, volunteers, referrals, follow-up and impact metrics. Current proposal material correctly labels the system private demo / early access and does not imply public availability or partner approval.

### 4.2 OCLS Community Navigation + Digital Opportunity Pilot — ARTIFACT-VERIFIED PROPOSAL FRUIT

The 2026-08-07 OCLS proposal explicitly positions ATLAS as a coordination/follow-up layer that complements—rather than replaces—Library Social Workers, Melrose programs and community partners. It does not imply OCLS approval.

### 4.3 Participant Identity & Consent — GREEN FRUIT

A canonical participant record should store only the identity/contact/context needed for an explicit service-navigation purpose, with consent scope, language, communication preference and revocation/retention rules.

**Invariant:** participants are not CRM leads by default.

### 4.4 Minimum Intake & Needs — GREEN FRUIT

The preferred journey begins with check-in/language, minimum consent and priority need. Intake should support configurable need categories without forcing excessive personal narratives.

### 4.5 Verified Resource Directory — GREEN FRUIT

Resources must include source/owner, eligibility summary, geography, languages, hours/capacity where known, last verification date and contact/official link. Expired/stale resources must be visible as such.

### 4.6 Referral / Closed-Loop Handoff — GREEN FRUIT

A mature handoff records participant consent, resource/partner, referral date, acknowledgement where available, next step and outcome status without copying unnecessary external case files.

### 4.7 Appointments & Capacity — GREEN CROSS-BRANCH FRUIT

Community may compose Operations scheduling/tasks and partner-provided capacity. Appointment records must identify whether ATLAS controls the slot or merely links/refers to an external provider.

### 4.8 Opt-in Case Follow-up — GREEN FRUIT

Follow-up should be time-limited, consent-aware and purpose-specific. Closed cases should not remain indefinitely active simply to improve engagement metrics.

### 4.9 Digital Opportunity / Skills Passport — ARTIFACT-VERIFIED PROPOSAL + GREEN RUNTIME

The OCLS proposal defines skills pathways, resumes/portfolios, headshots, pitch videos, maker/VR exploration and micro-credentials. Mature implementation should reuse Knowledge/Training/Skills instead of creating a parallel education ledger.

### 4.10 Employment & Reintegration Support — GREEN CROSS-BRANCH FRUIT

ATLAS may help with resumes, interview preparation, verified opportunities and skills pathways. Employment support should connect to HR/Knowledge only with appropriate participant consent and must not expose a vulnerable-person case history to employers.

### 4.11 Entrepreneurship Support — GREEN CROSS-BRANCH FRUIT

Business-plan, budgeting, basic accounting, creative production and pitch support may reuse Knowledge, Creator and Finance educational capabilities. Community assistance does not automatically create a commercial customer account.

### 4.12 Opportunity Studio — ARTIFACT-VERIFIED PROPOSAL FRUIT

The OCLS proposal defines appointment blocks for headshots, video resumes, portfolios, product photography and pitch videos with accessibility standards.

### 4.13 Resource Fair OS — ARTIFACT-VERIFIED PROPOSAL FRUIT

Concept includes QR check-in, partner capacity, appointments/queues, language support, follow-up survey and next-step tracking.

### 4.14 Mobile Community Lab — ARTIFACT-VERIFIED PROPOSAL FRUIT

Concept includes tablets/mobile stops for library signup, digital literacy, benefits navigation, employment and appointments.

### 4.15 Donation Inventory — GREEN CROSS-BRANCH FRUIT

In-kind donations should reuse canonical Inventory movement logic with donor/sponsor privacy controls and community-specific categories/condition/distribution semantics.

### 4.16 Volunteer Coordination — GREEN CROSS-BRANCH FRUIT

Volunteer identity/skills/shifts should reuse People + Operations where appropriate. Volunteer access to participant data follows role-specific least privilege and safeguarding requirements.

### 4.17 Donors, Sponsors & Grants — GREEN FRUIT

Sponsor/grant relationships and budgets must be separated from participant case records. Financial accounting belongs to Finance; Community owns program/objective/outcome context.

### 4.18 Sponsor / Grant Impact Ledger — GREEN FRUIT

The OCLS proposal explicitly calls for budget, in-kind value, sponsored equipment, outcomes and grant-objective traceability without mixing private participant records.

**System model:** derived impact records + Finance references + anonymous/aggregated program metrics.

### 4.19 Anonymous Impact Metrics — GREEN FRUIT

Metrics may include acknowledged referral handoffs, lookup time, completed skills pathways, participation and opt-in satisfaction. Aggregate reporting must avoid re-identification and should retain denominator/definition/time-window provenance.

### 4.20 Partner Portal — GREEN FRUIT

Community service partners may publish/verify resources, capacity and handoff status under scoped permissions. A partner does not automatically receive participant details beyond the consented handoff.

### 4.21 Health / Emotional Support Navigation — PARTNER-BOUND / GREEN

ATLAS can provide verified navigation and wellness education. Diagnosis, prescribing, therapy/medical care and clinical records remain with qualified licensed professionals/partners. The OCLS proposal explicitly says no diagnosis/treatment and no clinical-record storage.

### 4.22 Housing / Benefits / Immigration / Social-Service Navigation — PARTNER-BOUND / GREEN

ATLAS may organize verified information and referrals, but eligibility determinations and professional/legal services remain with authorized agencies/providers. Resource source and verification dates must remain visible.

### 4.23 Document / ID Readiness Assistance — GREEN CROSS-BRANCH FRUIT

ATLAS may provide checklists, printing/scanning guidance and official links. The proposal explicitly states that ATLAS does not store ID copies by default. Any future storage requires a separately justified Documents/Vault consent/security contract.

### 4.24 AI Community Navigator — GREEN FRUIT

The OCLS proposal defines a multilingual assistant grounded only in approved resources, displaying sources and handing sensitive/uncertain cases to a person.

**Invariant:** zero unsupported resource answers is a quality target; AI confidence never substitutes for source verification.

### 4.25 Staff Copilot + Toolkit — GREEN FRUIT

Verified internal resource knowledge, service scripts, translations, accessibility and referral/de-escalation guidance should be human-reviewed and permissioned.

### 4.26 Accessible Wayfinding — GREEN CROSS-BRANCH FRUIT

QR audio, large print, bilingual/plain-language instructions, lower-stimulation routes and consistent signage can compose Mobility/Maps/Global Context without exposing private case data.

### 4.27 Civic Memory / Story Lab — GREEN FRUIT

Community histories/oral stories must be consent-first, purpose-scoped and revocable where appropriate, with separate publication permission from service-navigation records.

### 4.28 Cyber + AI Literacy Clinics — ARTIFACT-VERIFIED PROPOSAL / GREEN PROGRAM

The proposal includes workshops on phishing, deepfakes, digital fraud, privacy and responsible AI. Curriculum belongs to Knowledge; Community owns outreach/participation context.

### 4.29 Foundation / Legal-Tax Structure — PARTNER-BOUND / ORGANIZATIONAL

Historical United Hands materials explicitly state that tax-deductible donation claims must not be made until legal/tax structure is completed. Formation, exemption status, charitable solicitation and tax reporting require appropriate legal/tax compliance.

### 4.30 Public Program Delivery Capacity — GREEN / PARTNER-BOUND

Food, hygiene, medical, counseling, transportation, housing and other services may be delivered directly only where organization capacity, safety, insurance, licensing and legal requirements permit. Otherwise ATLAS coordinates qualified partners/referrals.

## 5. Current WORKING FRUIT status in canonical repo

No dedicated Community participant/case-management runtime at Finance/HR/Operations maturity is currently evidenced in canonical `main`. The strongest current evidence is **ARTIFACT-VERIFIED** proposal/program architecture plus reusable Forms, Documents, Inventory, Operations, Knowledge, People and Finance foundations.

This is intentional: a well-developed proposal is valuable evidence, but it is not proof that a participant-data platform or public program is live.

## 6. Role / identity separation

| Role | Purpose | Data boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Participant / beneficiary | receives navigation/support | minimum necessary service context; never marketing lead by default |
| Volunteer | provides approved service | volunteer profile/skills/shift; participant access only as assigned |
| Donor / sponsor | funds/provides resources | contribution/program reporting; no private participant histories |
| Provider / partner | supplies verified external resource/service | resource/capacity/handoff data; participant detail only per consent |
| Staff / navigator | coordinates service journey | role-scoped participant/resource access |
| Public/report reader | sees impact | aggregated/de-identified metrics only |

## 7. Fruit chains

`Check-in/language → minimum consent → priority need → verified resource/skill pathway → appointment/handoff → opt-in follow-up → anonymous outcome`

`Donation → inventory receipt → controlled distribution → program reference → Finance/impact reporting`

`Volunteer → identity/skill verification → shift/task → scoped service interaction → completion/safeguarding evidence`

`Grant objective → budget/Finance references → program activities → de-identified outcomes → sponsor/grant report`

## 8. Community invariants

1. A person seeking help is not a CRM lead by default.
2. Participant data is minimum-necessary, purpose-bound and consent-aware.
3. Participant histories are separated from donor/sponsor/grant reporting.
4. Follow-up is opt-in/purpose-specific where appropriate and can be closed.
5. Resource recommendations identify source and verification freshness.
6. AI Navigator uses approved sources and human handoff for sensitive/uncertain cases.
7. Community health support is navigation/referral unless qualified providers deliver care.
8. ID copies are not stored by default.
9. Volunteer access to participant data is role-scoped and safeguarded.
10. Tax-deductibility is never claimed before legal/tax eligibility is established.
11. Proposal partner names do not imply approval or active partnership.
12. Public impact metrics are aggregated/de-identified and definition-traceable.
13. Community technology complements institutions/professionals rather than pretending to replace them.

## 10. Definition of ripe fruit

A Community fruit becomes **RIPE** only when role separation, consent, minimum-necessary data, verified resources, safeguarding, partner authority, retention, audit, accessibility and exact deployed program capacity are proven. A proposal, flyer or public message never by itself proves that a partner approved the project, a service is available or a donation is tax-deductible.
