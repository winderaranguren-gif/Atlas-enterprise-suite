# ATLAS Recovery Operations — B&P Auto Recovery Service Inc.

## Commercial Position
B&P Auto Recovery Service Inc. is registered in ATLAS as a prospect/future client.

ATLAS proposes a full recovery-operations platform covering dispatch, vehicle intake, lot management, personal-property control, customer appointments, releases, communications, fleet, drivers, lenders, payments, accounting, audit, documents, analytics, and ATLAS Intelligence.

## Core Workflow
Assignment → Locate → Recover → Photograph → Intake → Personal Property Inventory → Lot → Lender/Client Status → Release Authorization → Customer Appointment → Payment/Fees → Vehicle/Property Release → Close → Accounting/Audit.

## Included Capabilities
- Recovery Dispatch
- Vehicle Intake & Condition Evidence
- VIN / plate / odometer capture
- Personal Property Vault with photos and chain of custody
- Lot / storage location management
- Release authorization workflow
- Appointment scheduling and queue management
- Customer portal and notifications
- Lender / finance-company portal
- Fleet and driver management
- Towing / recovery unit maintenance
- Fees, receipts, payments and charge controls
- CRM / case tracking
- Documents and evidence management
- Audit log and permissions
- Accounting: AR, AP, GL, revenue by recovery/storage/towing, unit costs and profitability
- Multi-location operations
- Dashboards and KPI reporting
- ATLAS Suggestions and Intelligence
- ATLAS Multi-Pane Workspace
- English-first UI with Spanish language option
- Voice commands / Hey ATLAS
- Self-repair and health monitoring where safe

## Proposed Consideration / Settlement Concept
ATLAS may be offered with no initial software-license charge as part of a negotiated business settlement.

The requested consideration would be a written credit, waiver, or satisfaction of any recovery, towing, storage, release, administrative, or related charges that B&P Auto Recovery Service Inc. is legally authorized to waive or credit.

Any delinquent automobile-loan payments, principal, interest, lender-imposed fees, deficiency balance, reinstatement amount, or other debt controlled by the lender/finance company are outside B&P's authority unless B&P has explicit written authority from that creditor.

No debt should be treated as waived until the authorized party signs a written agreement identifying:
- the exact amount being credited or waived;
- the specific obligation being satisfied;
- the effective date;
- the ATLAS deliverables being exchanged;
- ownership/license terms;
- implementation scope;
- support period;
- confidentiality and data-protection terms;
- acceptance/sign-off procedure;
- confirmation that no additional amount remains due for the obligation being settled, if that is the agreement.

## ATLAS Delivery Principle
This proposal describes the intended commercial scope. A module is not represented as production-ready until its functionality, persistence, permissions, auditability, security, and end-to-end workflow have been verified.
